# 📚 My Reading List

**Track every book you want to read, are reading, or have finished, synced across all your devices.**

👉 **Try it live: [www.myreadinglist.online](https://www.myreadinglist.online)**

---

## ✨ Features

- 🔎 **Search millions of books** from Open Library, with an automatic Internet Archive fallback when Open Library is down
- 📖 **Pick the exact edition** you own, with covers, details and a full-size cover viewer
- 🗂️ **Organize your lists**: Plan to Read, Reading and Read, with quick Undo for mistakes
- 🔥 **Discover** trending books (today, this week, this month) and personal recommendations based on your lists
- ☁️ **Sync between PC and mobile** with a free account, using Google or email
- 🔁 **Open Library integration**: two-way shelf sync, or a one-time import of your public reading log
- 👤 **No account needed to start**: guest books stay in your browser and merge into your account when you sign in
- 📱 **Works on desktop and mobile**

## 🛠️ Built with

[Astro](https://astro.build) · TypeScript · [Clerk](https://clerk.com) (accounts) · [Neon](https://neon.tech) Postgres (cloud sync) · [Vercel](https://vercel.com) (hosting) · [Open Library](https://openlibrary.org) & [Internet Archive](https://archive.org) APIs
